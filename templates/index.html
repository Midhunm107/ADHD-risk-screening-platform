ADHD Insight

A research prototype that combines a short questionnaire with browser based attention tasks to produce a preliminary screening indication of ADHD-related behavioural patterns.

This is not a diagnosis. Results describe a behavioural profile only and are not a substitute for assessment by a qualified clinician. If you have concerns about ADHD, please speak with a healthcare professional.
