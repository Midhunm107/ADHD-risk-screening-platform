Before You Begin

Purpose

This assessment is part of an academic research project exploring whether a short questionnaire and browser based attention tasks can produce a useful preliminary screening indication of ADHD-related behavioural patterns.

What happens next

  1. Questionnaire
  2. Attention Task
  3. Go/No-Go Task
  4. Behavioural Analysis

This platform is developed for academic research and preliminary screening research. It does not provide a medical diagnosis and should not replace assessment by a qualified healthcare professional.
